Emir Abdelkader {1808-26/05/1883}= A prominent Algerian military and political leader who resisted the French colonization of Algeria. Born in Mascara, son of Muhieddine, he unified Algerian tribes and led a 15-year resistance against France. He was captured and exiled, later released by Napoleon III. He is considered the founder of the modern Algerian state.
Larbi Ben M'hidi {1923-04/03/1957}= Algerian prominent figure and founding member of FLN. Born in Ain M'lila, he played a key role in organizing the revolution in Oran and later Algiers. He was arrested by French paratroopers during the Battle of Algiers and killed in captivity.
Abane Ramdane {1920-27/12/1957}= A central figure in the Algerian Revolution known as the architect of the Soummam Congress of 1956. Born in Azouza Kabylie, he shaped the political structure of the FLN. He was assassinated by members of his own movement in Morocco.
Didouche Mourad {1927-18/01/1955}= One of the nine historic leaders who launched the Algerian Revolution on November 1 1954. Born in Algiers, he commanded the Constantine region during the early days of the war. He was killed in battle near Conde-Smendou just months after the revolution began.
Mostefa Ben Boulaïd {1917-22/03/1956}= One of the nine historic founders of the FLN and commander of the Aures region. Born in Arris, he was a farmer and veteran of World War II who became a key military leader. He died when a booby-trapped radio package exploded in his hands.
Krim Belkacem {1922-18/10/1970}= A major leader of the Algerian Revolution and chief negotiator of the Evian Accords of 1962. Born in Ait Yahia Moussa Kabylie, he was a guerrilla fighter since 1947. He signed the ceasefire that ended French rule. He was later strangled in a Frankfurt hotel room.
Mohamed Boudiaf {1919-29/06/1992}= One of the historic leaders of the FLN and co-founder of the Algerian Revolution. Born in M'Sila, he was a key organizer of the November 1st uprising. He returned in 1992 as Head of State but was assassinated during a public speech in Annaba.
Houari Boumediene {1932-27/12/1978}= President of Algeria from 1965 to 1978. Born in Guelma, he led a military coup against Ahmed Ben Bella. He nationalized Algerian oil industries in 1971 and pursued socialist economic policies. He died in office of a rare blood disease.
Ahmed Ben Bella {1916-11/04/2012}= The first President of independent Algeria serving from 1963 to 1965. Born in Maghnia, he was one of the nine historic leaders of the FLN. He was overthrown in a coup by Houari Boumediene and spent years under house arrest before returning to political life.
Ferhat Abbas {1899-1985}= A prominent Algerian nationalist politician and the first President of the Provisional Government of the Algerian Republic. Born in Taher Jijel, he initially advocated for assimilation before embracing full independence. He served as president of the National Assembly after independence.
Fatima N'Soumer {1830-09/07/1863}= A female Berber resistance leader from Kabylie who fought against French colonization. Born in Ait Mahmoud, she led fierce battles in the Jurjura mountains. She was captured in 1857 and died in captivity in Tablat. She is a national symbol of Algerian resistance.
Messali Hadj {1898-03/06/1974}= Founder of the first Algerian nationalist movement and considered the father of Algerian nationalism. Born in Tlemcen, he created the Etoile Nord-Africaine in 1926 and later the PPA and MTLD parties. His legacy in shaping Algerian nationalism is immense.
Hassiba Ben Bouali {1938-08/10/1957}= A young female militant of the FLN who fought in the Battle of Algiers alongside Ali la Pointe. Born in Hadjout, she hid in the Casbah and was killed when French forces blew up the building where she was hiding. She became a symbol of female sacrifice in the revolution.
Ali Ammar {1930-08/10/1957}= Known as Ali la Pointe, a famous urban guerrilla fighter during the Battle of Algiers. Born in Miliana, he was recruited by the FLN and became one of the most hunted militants by French paratroopers. He was killed when his hideout in the Casbah was dynamited.
Yacef Saadi {1928-2021}= Commander of the FLN in Algiers during the Battle of Algiers in 1956 and 1957. Born in the Casbah of Algiers, he organized the urban resistance network. He was captured alive by French forces. After independence he became a senator and film producer.
Mohamed Khider {1912-04/01/1967}= One of the nine historic leaders of the FLN and a founding member of the Algerian Revolution. Born in Biskra, he was an Algerian representative in the French parliament before joining the revolution. He was assassinated in Madrid under mysterious circumstances.
Benyoucef Benkhedda {1920-2003}= President of the Provisional Government of the Algerian Republic from 1961 to 1962. Born in Eucalyptus near Algiers, he led the final negotiations of the Evian Accords. He later opposed the post-independence government policies.
Lamine Debaghine {1917-2003}= A doctor and nationalist politician and member of the MTLD and later the FLN. Born in Skikda, he served in the Provisional Government and advocated for Algerian independence at international forums including the United Nations.
Navarino battle {20/10/1827}: A naval battle between the Ottoman Empire and allied forces of Britain France and Russia that weakened Ottoman control over Algeria and paved the way for the French invasion.
French invasion of Algeria {05/07/1830}: French forces landed in Sidi Fredj and captured Algiers marking the beginning of 132 years of French colonization of Algeria.
Emir Abdelkader resistance {1832-1847}: Emir Abdelkader led a prolonged armed resistance against French colonization unifying Algerian tribes and establishing a proto-state before surrendering to French forces.
Setif massacre {08/05/1945}: French forces massacred thousands of Algerian civilians in Setif Guelma and Kherrata following demonstrations celebrating the end of World War II. Estimates range from 8000 to 45000 killed.
November 1 Revolution {01/11/1954}: The Algerian War of Independence began when the FLN launched coordinated attacks across Algeria marking the start of an eight-year war against French colonialism.
Soummam Congress {20/08/1956}: A landmark meeting held in the Soummam valley in Kabylie where FLN leaders adopted a political and military charter that organized and unified the revolution.
Battle of Algiers {1956-1957}: An intense urban guerrilla conflict fought between the FLN and French paratroopers in the Casbah of Algiers where French forces used torture to dismantle the FLN network.
Evian Accords {18/03/1962}: Peace negotiations between France and the FLN held in Evian-les-Bains France resulting in a ceasefire agreement that ended the Algerian War of Independence.
Independence of Algeria {05/07/1962}: Algeria officially gained independence from France after 132 years of colonization following a referendum in which the overwhelming majority voted for independence.
Nationalisation of Oil {24/02/1971}: President Houari Boumediene nationalized the Algerian oil industry taking control from French companies marking a major step in full economic independence.
Black October {05/10/1988}: Mass protests erupted across Algeria demanding political and economic reforms. The army crackdown killed over 500 people leading to the constitutional reforms of 1989 and multiparty politics.
Civil war begins {1992}: Algeria entered a decade-long civil conflict after the military cancelled the 1991 elections which the Islamic Salvation Front FIS was poised to win. Tens of thousands were killed in the violence.
